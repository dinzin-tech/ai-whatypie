1. Overview

The Click To WhatsApp Ads module is a powerful integration that lets you manage your Facebook advertising assets and launch high-converting WhatsApp campaigns directly from WhatyPie. By bridging Facebook's global reach with WhatsApp's personal engagement, you can drive more sales and provides instant customer support.

Asset Synchronization

Automatically sync your Facebook Pages and Ad Accounts to maintain up-to-date marketing assets.

Guided Campaign Creation

Follow our 3-step wizard to define objectives, target audiences, and design engaging ad creatives.

Real-time Analytics

Monitor spend, reach, and conversion metrics through a comprehensive performance dashboard.

2. Asset Management (Sync Social Accounts)

Before launching campaigns, you must synchronize your Facebook assets. WhatyPie provides a unified view of your connected Pages and Ad Accounts.

Tip: Use the "Sync Ads" button periodically to ensure any changes made directly in Facebook Ads Manager are reflected in WhatyPie.
Key Management Features:
  • Account Filtering: Quickly find specific ad accounts using the integrated search bar.
  • Status Monitoring: Instantly check if an account is Active and if it has a valid payment method.
  • Direct Action: Click "Manage Campaigns" to jump straight into the campaign hierarchy for that specific account.

3. Campaign Hierarchy Management

Manage your ads using the standard Facebook hierarchy: Campaigns → Ad Sets → Ads. This structured approach allows for granular control over budgets and targeting.

Campaigns Level

View high-level objectives (e.g., Awareness, Sales) and set global budgets.

Ad Sets Level

Manage demographic targeting, placements, and specific ad set statuses.

Ads (Creative) Level

Review and edit specific ad creatives, preview visual appearance, and monitor individual performance.

4. Creating a Click To WhatsApp Campaign

Our guided 3-step wizard simplifies the complex Facebook ad creation process.

STEP 1: Campaign Information

Set your foundational campaign parameters.

  • Campaign Name: A descriptive name for tracking (e.g., Summer Sale 2026).
  • Objective: Choose Traffic or Sales to drive WhatsApp messages.
  • Daily Budget: Control your spend by setting a daily limit in USD/INR.
STEP 2: Targeting Configuration

Define exactly who should see your ads.

  • Location Targeting: Use the interactive map to select countries or regions.
  • Demographics: Refine by Age range (e.g., 18-55) and Gender.
  • Platforms: Select where your ads appear (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger).
STEP 3: Creative & Welcome Experience

Design the ad and the initial user conversation.

Welcome Experience

Define the greeting text and pre-filled message a user sees when they click your ad. Connect an Automation Trigger to start a Chatbot flow automatically.

5. Performance Analytics Dashboard

Optimize your ROI by monitoring campaign performance through our real-time analytics engine.

Metric Overview

Track Total Spend, Impressions, Reach, and Link Clicks in a clean, card-based interface.

Audience Demographics

Visualize the gender and age distribution of your audience to refine future targeting.

PRO TIP: Check the "Platform Breakdown" chart to see whether Facebook or Instagram is driving more traffic, and adjust your budget allocation accordingly.
