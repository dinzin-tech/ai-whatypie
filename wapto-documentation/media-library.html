Centralized Asset Management

The Media Library is your business's centralized digital vault for WhatsApp communication. It allows you to upload, store, and organize images, videos, and documents, ensuring that your team can respond to customer inquiries with high-quality media in seconds.

Asset Reuse
Save time by reusing promotional videos and product brochures across multiple chats.
Instant Response
Access frequently used media directly from the chat interface for lightning-fast support.
Organized Storage
Maintain a professional repository of all business documents, invoices, and assets.

1. Access & Upload Workflow

Follow these steps to populate your library with essential business assets.

Sidebar Navigation: Navigate to the Media section from the primary sidebar.
Initiate Upload: Click the + Add New or Upload Media button.
Drag & Drop: Select files from your device. We support a wide range of formats (JPG, PNG, MP4, PDF).
Finalize: Once uploaded, the file is instantly available in your Media Library for all agents.

2. Sending Media via Chat

Enhance your conversations by attaching media directly from your library during active chats.

Open Inbox: Navigate to any active contact or chat.
Trigger Attachment: Click the Attachment/Media icon in the message input area.
Select Library: Choose Media Library from the pop-up menu.
Choose & Send: Select your file from the modal and click Send.

Supported Media Types

Images

High-resolution JPG and PNG files for product showcases.

Videos

MP4 files for tutorials, demos, and promotional clips.

Documents

PDF and DOCX for invoices, brochures, and contracts.

Business Use Cases
Send automated invoices or documents instantly.
Share brochures or product PDFs during discovery calls.
Provide helpful tutorial videos for support queries.
Best Practices

File Naming: Use clear, optimized file names (e.g., Price_List_2026.pdf) for easier searching.

Regular Cleanup: Delete outdated media files to keep your library organized and current.

Troubleshooting

Upload Failed?
Check if the file exceeds size limits (15MB for video, 5MB for images).
Media Not Sending?
Verify your internet connection and WhatsApp account permissions.
Missing Files?
Try refreshing the page or checking if the file is in a supported format.
